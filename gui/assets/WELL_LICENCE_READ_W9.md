# WELL Licence Read — Wave 9 Build 1

Own code, standard library only — in fact, the wellbeing
module imports nothing beyond ``__future__`` annotations,
and the tools coordinator imports only ``json``,
``pathlib``, and the wellbeing module itself.

No third-party code, package, model, dataset, font, sound
pack, or feed is introduced by this build. No health data
is collected, measured, or inferred, so no health-data
licence or terms question is engaged. No medical claim is
made anywhere in the module, the tools, or their
descriptions.

Nothing here is a medical device, a diagnosis, a
treatment, or a health score, and nothing in this build
may be described as one.
